X or Y: Logic Puzzle: Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 November 2026 · Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game X or Y: Logic Puzzle (the “game”, package com.xory.xorygrid) uses, where it is stored and how you can delete it. The game is published on Google Play by XORY Apps, an independent developer based in India (“we”, “us”).

The short version: the game has no ads and no analytics or tracking of our own, there is no account to create with us, and we don’t run any servers. Your progress is stored on your phone. The game uses Google Play Games Services, which is run by Google: Google’s Play Games software collects some diagnostic and analytics data whenever the game runs, and if you sign in, a copy of your progress is saved in your Google Play Games account. That data goes to Google. We never receive it.

1. Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves the following on your phone, in storage that only the game can access:

  • your progress: level, skill rating, number of puzzles completed, which puzzles you have solved, and whether you have finished the tutorial and warm-up;
  • your daily-puzzle streak, streak freezes and best streak;
  • your settings: theme, vibration, reduce motion, auto-✕;
  • any puzzle you left unfinished, so you can continue it.

None of this includes your name, email address, contacts, location or any identifier for your device. The game doesn’t send this information to us.

2. Android backup

If backup is turned on for your device, Android automatically backs up the game’s saved files to your own Google account, so your progress can come back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This is a feature of Android, controlled by your device’s backup settings. On Android 9 and later with a screen lock, the backup is encrypted so that only your devices can read it. We have no access to these backups.

3. Google Play Games Services

The game includes Google Play Games Services, which is provided by Google and handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Whenever the game runs, whether or not you sign in, the Play Games software starts with it. Google says this software collects some data automatically, such as analytics and diagnostic information used to keep the service stable and improve it, and that it encrypts data in transit (see Google’s description). This data goes to Google, not to us.

If you are signed in to Google Play Games (automatically, if your Play Games settings allow it, or by tapping Sign in in the game’s Settings), the game also uses your Play Games account to save a copy of your progress (level, skill rating, puzzles completed, puzzles solved and daily streak) to Google’s Saved Games service, and reads it back to keep your devices in step. Your settings and unfinished puzzles are not uploaded. Your Play Games account belongs to Google; the game doesn’t create an account of its own.

The game doesn’t read your Play Games name, picture, email address or friends list. The only Play Games data it reads is your own saved progress for this game, and it reads it on your device.

You don’t have to sign in. Every puzzle, the daily puzzle and all progress work without Play Games, and without an internet connection.

4. What we do not do

  • We don’t show ads, and we don’t use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t add analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of our own. (What Google’s Play Games software collects is described in section 3.)
  • We don’t sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We don’t have accounts, servers or databases of our own, so we hold no information about you.

Google Play gives every developer aggregated statistics (such as install counts) and crash and performance reports from users who have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google. These reports don’t identify you to us.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

5. Deleting your data

  • On your phone: open the game’s Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the game. Uninstalling removes all of the game’s files from your phone.
  • Google Play Games: you can delete this game’s saved data, or your whole Play Games account, from your Play Games profile or the Play Games settings on your phone, or from your Google Account. Resetting progress in the game while signed in also replaces the cloud copy with a fresh start.
  • Android backup: manage or delete device backups in your Google account’s backup settings (for example in Google One, or under Settings → Google → Backup on your phone).
  • Questions or help: email us at sahilmohindroo@gmail.com. Because we don’t hold any of your data ourselves, we will explain how to delete it with the tools above.

6. Children

The game isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. We collect nothing ourselves; the only data that leaves the device goes to Google, as described in section 3. If you believe a child has used the game and you want their data removed, email sahilmohindroo@gmail.com and we will help you remove it with the tools in section 5.

7. Security

Your data stays on your device except for what Google Play Games and Android backup handle, and both are Google services that encrypt data in transit. The game has no permission to access the internet directly; the only permission it requests is vibration. The Privacy policy link in the game’s Settings opens this page in your browser.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information in a new way, for example if ads or analytics are added in a future version, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
